MATTERMIND v0

Learns from your lab results and suggests which formulations to test next.

What this is and isn't. It knows no materials science. It only learns patterns in the results you enter, then suggests experiments. The sample data below is synthetic and invented to show the workflow. Never treat its output as a tested or safe material.

1. Your experiments

One row per formulation. Header row first, comma-separated. The last column is the measured result. Ingredient columns can be any numbers (e.g. percentages that sum to 100).

Method: Gaussian-process regression with a simple RBF kernel. Suggested recipes are blends of recipes you have already tested, so ingredient totals stay consistent. The model check below uses leave-one-out error: if it is not clearly better than guessing the average, add more experiments before trusting any suggestion.
